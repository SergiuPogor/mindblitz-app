Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09 · Version 2.1

This policy explains how the MindBlitz Android app, its website at https://sergiupogor.github.io/mindblitz-app/ and our support email handle information. MindBlitz is provided by MEDIAPRONET SRL (Societatea cu Răspundere Limitată MEDIAPRONET), a limited liability company registered in the Republic of Moldova with company ID (IDNO) 1012600005500 ("we", "us"), represented by its CEO, Serghei Pogor. Contact: mediapronetsrl@gmail.com.

The short version

  • MindBlitz runs on your phone and works offline. The app itself has no permission to use the internet.
  • There are no MindBlitz accounts, no ads and no tracking.
  • Optional: if you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is kept in your Google account as a cloud save, so you can get it back on a new phone. Google runs this service; we cannot see your save.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share personal data through the app.
  • Optional purchases are paid through Google Play. We never see your payment details, and your purchases are checked on your phone.
  • Your progress stays on your phone (and in your Play Games cloud save, if you use it) until you delete it. See Delete Your Data.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

For the little personal data we do process (support emails, order records from Google Play and website hosting, described below), the controller is MEDIAPRONET SRL, str. N. We have not appointed a data protection officer, because the law does not require one for what we do. Send any privacy question or request to the email address above.

What the app keeps on your phone

The app saves the following in its private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it. If you use the Play Games cloud save, the same progress is also copied to your Google account (see Google Play Games and cloud save):

  • Game scores, best scores, recent ratings, skill ratings, your Blitz IQ and its history, and your Brain Score
  • Daily Blitz results, your streak and an unfinished Blitz, so that it can resume
  • XP, Mind Level, badges and the stats shown in charts
  • IQ Challenge results, an unfinished test, and the name you type on the certificate, if you type one
  • Brain Teasers progress, stars and hints used
  • The player names and colours you last used in Duel
  • Your settings, such as sound, vibration and the reminder time
  • Your levels and stars in each game
  • An optional age range you choose stays on your device and only adjusts game pace (for example more time in timed games and how many levels start open). It is never sent anywhere and does not change your ratings or Blitz IQ. You can change it, or pick "Prefer not to say", on the Me tab at any time.
  • Whether you have Plus, your coin balance, the items you unlocked, and the purchase records Google Play passes to the app

Android's own libraries also keep a few technical records in the app's storage, such as the schedule for refreshing the home-screen widgets. Generated sound files and the last result picture you shared are kept in the app's cache folder, which Android may clear at any time.

This information is under your control. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it from your phone. It is never sent to us and we have no access to it, so we do not collect it or otherwise process it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not use the internet itself. The app has no INTERNET permission. Google Play services on your phone does the network part for Google Play billing and Play Games.
  • It has no MindBlitz accounts. The only sign-in is the optional Google Play Games one described below.
  • It shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software of ours. The Google Play Games library sends Google its own analytics and diagnostics, as described below.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It does not profile you or make automated decisions about you. Ratings, Blitz IQ, Brain Score, brain type and the IQ estimate are worked out on your phone, for the game only, and have no effect outside it.

Permissions

The app uses these Android permissions, all of which work only on the phone:

  • Vibration (VIBRATE): a short buzz on right and wrong answers. You can turn it off on the Me tab.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only for the daily reminder, and only if you turn it on. Android asks you first.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): sets the reminder and the widget refresh again after your phone restarts.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): lets the app ask the Google Play Store app on your phone to show a purchase screen and to report your purchases. It does not give the app internet access.
  • Keep awake, network state and foreground service (WAKE_LOCK, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by Android's WorkManager library, which keeps the home-screen widgets up to date. Network state only lets that library check whether a connection exists. None of these give the app internet access.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share, the app makes a result picture or text on your phone, and Android's share menu sends it to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share and choose an app. Once you post something to another service, that service's own terms and privacy policy apply, and we have no control over it.

Reminder and widgets

The daily reminder is optional and off until you turn it on. It is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us or to any server. The home-screen widgets show your streak and today's Blitz using the data already on your phone. You can turn the reminder off in the app and remove the widgets from your home screen at any time.

Google Play Games and cloud save

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. The game works the same without it.

  • Signing in: you can sign in from the Cloud save card on the Me tab. If your phone already has a Play Games profile, Play Games may sign you in by itself when the app opens, and shows a "Welcome back" banner. Play Games then shares your Play Games gamer name and avatar with the app, which shows your name on the Cloud save card.
  • Cloud save: while you are signed in, the app saves a copy of the progress listed above (scores, streaks, XP, badges, settings, coins and the rest) in Google Play Games Saved Games, in your Google account. It updates the copy when you leave the app or tap Save now. On a new phone, signing in brings your progress back. If this phone and the cloud save both have different progress, the app asks you which one to keep.
  • Who runs it: Google provides Play Games as a separate controller, under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google encrypts this data in transit. We never receive your cloud save or your Google account details, and the app can only read and write its own save for the signed-in player. Google also collects analytics and diagnostics through the Play Games library to keep its service working, and keeps a record of your Play Games profile and activity under its own policy.
  • Turning it off and deleting it: you can manage or delete your Play Games profile and the data Google keeps for each game in the Google Play Games app or at https://play.google.com/games/profile. Deleting the app does not delete the cloud save. See Delete Your Data.

Device backups

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup, so that it comes back on a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google (or your device maker) under your own account and its privacy policy. We cannot access it. You can turn backup off, or delete a backup, in your phone's settings.

Google Play

We distribute MindBlitz through Google Play. Google processes information when you download, update or rate apps, as described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

  • Ratings: the app may ask Google Play to show its rating dialog. Google Play shows that dialog and handles your answer. We see only the public rating and review, as on any Play listing.
  • Crash and performance statistics: if you allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics, Google may show us totals and crash reports without names in Play Console. We do not use them to identify anyone.

Purchases

MindBlitz may offer an optional Plus subscription, a Plus Lifetime purchase and coin packs. Subscriptions and Purchases explains the terms.

  • Google Play handles payment: when you buy something, Google Play collects and processes your payment details and billing information as a separate controller, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice (https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice). We never receive your card or bank details.
  • On your phone: Google Play gives the app on your phone the product you bought, the purchase status and time, an order number and a purchase token. The app checks this on the phone and saves your Plus status and coin balance there. The app does not send any of it to us or to anyone else.
  • In Play Console: Google gives us limited records of each order, such as the order number, product, price, date, country or region and status. We use them only to handle refunds and support requests, to prevent fraud and to keep our accounts. The legal bases are performing our contract with you and meeting our accounting and tax duties (Article 6(1)(b) and (c) of the GDPR). We keep these records for as long as accounting and tax law requires, and then delete them.

When you email us

If you write to mediapronetsrl@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, your message and any attachments. We use them only to answer you and to deal with what you asked, such as a bug report or a privacy request.

The Feedback card on the Me tab (Report a bug, Suggest a feature, Send feedback) only opens your own email app with a draft addressed to us. The draft includes the app version, Android version, phone model and app language, so we can reproduce bugs. You see all of it and can edit or delete it, and nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. The app itself still sends nothing.

  • Legal basis: taking the steps you ask for, and our legitimate interest in answering messages and keeping a record of them (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) of the GDPR, and the matching rules of Moldovan law).
  • Email provider: our mailbox is run by Google (Gmail), which processes messages for us and may store them on servers outside the Republic of Moldova and the EU.
  • How long: we delete support emails within 24 months after the conversation ends, unless we need them longer to deal with a legal claim or because the law requires it.

Please do not send us sensitive information we have not asked for.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and loads no fonts, scripts or images from other companies.

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, whether or not the visitor has a GitHub account, and handles these logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We do not receive these logs. Our legal basis for using GitHub is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.
  • Your browser: the light or dark theme you pick, and your best score in the demo game, are saved in your browser's local storage only so that the page remembers them. They never leave your browser, and you can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Links: links to other websites, such as Google Play, take you to services with their own privacy policies.

International transfers

The app itself transfers no data to us. If you use the Play Games cloud save, Google stores it on its servers, which may be in the United States and other countries. Our email provider (Google) and our website host (GitHub) may process data in the United States and other countries. Both describe the safeguards they use for such transfers, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, in their privacy statements.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. If the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or Moldovan law applies to you, you also have the right to:

Children

MindBlitz is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, so it collects none from children. A name typed into the app stays on the phone and is never sent to us (it is part of the Play Games cloud save only if the player signs in to Play Games, which Google's own age rules govern). Purchases need a Google account, and parents can require approval for purchases in Google Play's parental controls. Security

Your progress stays in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. A Play Games cloud save is protected by your Google account, so keep that account secure too. Please keep your phone secure with a screen lock. Content you share outside the app is outside our control.
