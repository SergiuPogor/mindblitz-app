Subscriptions and Purchases

Last updated: 2026-11-09 · Version 1.1

This policy covers the optional paid extras in MindBlitz, offered by MEDIAPRONET SRL, Republic of Moldova: the MindBlitz Plus subscription, the one-time Plus Lifetime purchase, coin packs and Boost packs. It forms part of our Terms of Use. The games stay free to download and play. If paid extras are not yet shown in your version of the app, this policy applies once they are.

What you can buy

  • MindBlitz Plus (monthly or yearly): a subscription that unlocks the extras listed on the Plus screen in the app. The Plus screen shows exactly what is included before you buy.
  • Plus Lifetime: a single payment that unlocks the same extras as Plus, with no renewal.
  • Coin packs: coins are an in-app currency that you can spend on hints, streak freezes and cosmetic items, such as themes. You can also earn coins by playing, without paying.
  • Boost packs: a Boost is a one-use helper in a single game: it gives 10 more seconds, one more life or one more try. You also get some Boosts free, and Plus adds Boosts every month.

MindBlitz is a game for fun. Plus, coins, Boosts and their extras do not make any claim to improve memory, intelligence or any other ability.

Prices

Before you confirm a purchase, the app and the Google Play payment screen show the price, the currency and, for subscriptions, the billing period. The Google Play screen shows the final price, including any tax it adds. Prices can differ between countries.

Payment through Google Play

All purchases are made and paid through Google Play, using the payment method on your Google account, under the Google Play Terms of Service. We never see or store your card or bank details. Google Play tells the app on your phone that a purchase went through, and the app checks this on the phone. There is no MindBlitz account and no MindBlitz server.

Free trials

A subscription may start with a free trial. Before you confirm, Google Play shows how long the trial lasts and the date of the first charge. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged. If you do not cancel, the subscription starts and renews automatically at the price shown. A free trial may be offered only once per Google account.

Automatic renewal

Monthly and yearly Plus subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period, and Google Play charges your payment method, unless you cancel before the renewal date. Google Play shows your renewal date under Payments and subscriptions.

Plus Lifetime does not renew. It stays with your Google account for as long as we offer the app.

How to cancel

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose MindBlitz and tap Cancel subscription.

You can also cancel at https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage does not cancel a subscription. After you cancel, Plus stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and then stops. You keep your scores and progress.

Price changes

If we change the price of a subscription, Google Play tells you before the new price applies, as its rules require, and in some cases asks you to agree to it. You can cancel before the new price applies. A change never affects a period you have already paid for.

Coins

  • Coins are a limited, personal licence to use in-app items in MindBlitz. They have no value in real money, and you cannot cash them out, sell them, transfer them to another person, or exchange them for anything outside the app.
  • The app shows what each item costs in coins before you spend them, and each coin pack shows its price in your currency before you buy it.
  • Coins do not buy random rewards. You always know which item you get.
  • Coins do not expire while you keep the app installed.
  • Your coin balance is stored only on your phone. Coins are removed if you uninstall the app or clear its storage, and bought coins cannot be restored after that, because we have no server that keeps a copy. A device backup may bring your balance back on a new phone.
  • We may change the items on offer and their coin prices. We will not take away coins you already hold, except to correct an obvious error or a refunded or fraudulent purchase.

Boosts

  • Boosts follow the same rules as coins above: they have no value in real money, cannot be cashed out or transferred, do not buy random rewards, do not expire while you keep the app installed, and are stored only on your phone.
  • Each Boost is used up when you use it in a game.

Restoring purchases

Plus subscriptions and Plus Lifetime are linked to the Google account you bought them with. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, sign in to Google Play with the same Google account, open the Plus screen and tap Restore purchases. Coins are spent on the phone, so coin packs and Boost packs cannot be restored this way (see Coins above).

Refunds

Google Play handles payments, so most refund requests go through Google under its refund policy. You can ask for a refund in your Google Play order history; Google allows this within a short time after purchase. After that, email us with your Google Play order number, and we will look at your request fairly. We do not normally refund part of a subscription period, unless the law requires it.

Your 14-day right of withdrawal (EU, EEA and UK)

If you are a consumer in the European Union, the EEA or the United Kingdom, you have a right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days, without giving a reason, with these exceptions that the law allows:

  • Plus Lifetime, coin packs and Boost packs are digital content delivered at once. When you buy them, you ask for immediate delivery and accept that you lose your right of withdrawal once delivery starts. After that, you can no longer withdraw.
  • Plus subscriptions are a digital service. You can withdraw within 14 days of the day you subscribed. If you asked for Plus to start straight away, we may keep or charge an amount in proportion to the time you used it before you withdrew.

To withdraw, cancel the subscription in Google Play and email us a clear statement, for example: "I withdraw from my purchase of MindBlitz Plus, ordered on (date), Google Play order number (number), name, email." We refund any amount due within 14 days, through Google Play, using the payment method you used.

Your mandatory consumer rights, including rights if something you bought does not work as described, are not affected by this policy.

If something goes wrong

If Plus does not unlock, a purchase looks wrong or coins or Boosts are missing after a purchase, first tap Restore purchases on the Plus screen. If that does not help, email us with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA) and a short description. We will check it and make it right.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our offers or the law change. We will update the date at the top, and if a change is significant, tell you in the app before it applies. Changes never affect a period or a purchase you have already paid for.
