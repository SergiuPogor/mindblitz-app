Health and Safety

Last updated: 2026-11-07 · Version 1.0

Please read this notice before you play MindBlitz, by MEDIAPRONET SRL, and share it with anyone who plays on your phone.

Photosensitivity warning

A very small number of people may have a seizure when they see certain flashing lights or patterns, even if they have never had one before and have no history of epilepsy. Some MindBlitz games flash tiles, change colours quickly, or show moving dots and confetti.

  • If you or anyone in your family has epilepsy or has had seizures, talk to a doctor before you play.
  • Stop playing at once and see a doctor if you feel dizzy, have blurred or changed vision, eye or muscle twitches, a loss of awareness, confusion or any involuntary movement.
  • To lower the risk, play in a well-lit room, hold the phone at a comfortable distance, avoid playing when tired, and take a break of at least 10 minutes every hour.

Vibration and sound

The app gives a short vibration and plays sounds on right and wrong answers. Turn either off with the Vibration and Sound switches on the Me tab. Keep the volume at a comfortable level, especially with headphones.

Comfort and posture

Take regular breaks. If your hands, wrists, arms or eyes feel tired or sore, stop and rest. In Duel, two people tap the same phone: lay it on a steady surface and keep your fingers clear of each other. Do not play while walking, driving or doing anything that needs your attention.

For fun, not a medical tool

MindBlitz is a game for entertainment. It is not a medical device and does not test, diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. We do not claim that it improves memory, attention or intelligence, or that it slows or prevents age-related decline. If you are worried about your memory or thinking, please talk to a doctor.

The IQ estimate

The IQ Challenge gives a rough estimate for fun, from a short set of in-app puzzles. It is not a clinical IQ test, and you should not rely on it for any decision about yourself or anyone else. A real IQ test is given in person by a trained psychologist.

Blitz IQ

Blitz IQ is a game score from your answers in MindBlitz games, not a real IQ test. It starts at 100 for everyone, goes up with right answers and down with mistakes, and at the end of each game a pace check compares the game with the game's own targets (a game with mostly right answers never lowers it). It uses an IQ-like scale only because the numbers are easy to read: it does not measure intelligence, it is not compared with other people, and playing to raise it does not make you more intelligent. The IQ Challenge estimate may move it a little, and that estimate is also only for fun. Practice, Party, Duel and friends' challenges do not change it.

Children

MindBlitz is meant for people aged 13 and over. If a younger child plays on your phone, please supervise them and keep this notice in mind.
