Playing safely

Some games use flashing tiles, quick colour changes, confetti and vibration. If you have epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing lights, talk to a doctor before you play, and stop at once if you feel unwell. Take regular breaks. In Duel, two people tap the same phone, so lay it on a steady surface and keep your fingers clear of each other. See Health and Safety for the full notice.
