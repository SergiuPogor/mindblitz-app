Your brain profile

Your Blitz IQ, a game score that starts at 100 and moves with every answer, plus a rating for each skill, a radar chart and a brain type such as Brilliant Detective to share. Blitz IQ is not a real IQ test.

Private by design

Your scores never leave your phone

MindBlitz works offline and does not have permission to use the internet. There is no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone unless you choose to sign in with Google Play Games for a cloud save, or tap Share and pick an app. This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies.

Questions

Good to know

Does MindBlitz cost anything?

MindBlitz is free to download and play, with no ads. Optional extras, such as MindBlitz Plus and coin packs, may be offered through Google Play. See Subscriptions and Purchases.

Do I need an account or internet?

Neither. Everything runs on your phone. You only need a connection to share your result in another app or to buy something on Google Play.

Is the IQ estimate real?

It is a rough estimate for fun, not a clinical IQ test. A real IQ test is given in person by a trained psychologist.

What is Blitz IQ?

Blitz IQ is a game score from your answers in MindBlitz games. It starts at 100, goes up with right answers and down with mistakes. It is not a real IQ test and does not measure intelligence.

Will MindBlitz make me smarter?

MindBlitz is a game made for fun. We do not claim that it improves memory, intelligence or any other ability, or that it prevents any condition. Your scores usually rise as you get used to each game.

Some games flash. Is that safe?

Some games use flashing tiles and quick colour changes. If you have epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing lights, talk to a doctor before you play. Read the Health and Safety notice.

When does a new Daily Blitz start?

At midnight on your phone's clock. Everyone gets the same five puzzles that day.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones with Android 8.0 or newer.

How do I delete my data?

Your progress is on your phone, and in your Google account only if you signed in to Play Games for a cloud save. Uninstall the app or clear its storage, and delete the cloud save from your Play Games profile. See Delete Your Data.
