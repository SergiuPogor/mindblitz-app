Help and Support

Last updated: 2026-11-09 · Version 2.0

Need help? Open the Me tab and use the Feedback card to report a bug or suggest a feature, or email mediapronetsrl@gmail.com. The Feedback card fills in the details below for you. It helps to include your phone model, your Android version and the app version, which is shown at the bottom of the Me tab. We read every message and reply as soon as we can, in English or Romanian.

Which phones does MindBlitz run on?

Android phones with Android 8.0 or newer. The app works fully offline and does not need an internet connection.

Is the Daily Blitz the same for everyone?

Yes. Every player gets the same five games with the same puzzles each day. A new Blitz starts at midnight on your phone's clock.

I lost my streak

Your streak counts days in a row on which you finished the Daily Blitz. It stays alive until the end of the day after your last Blitz. Turn on the daily reminder on the Me tab so you do not miss a day.

How is my Brain Score worked out?

Each game turns your score into a rating from 0 to 100 against the game's own target. A skill's rating is the average of your last five ratings in that skill, and your Brain Score, from 0 to 1000, averages your five skills. It changes as you play, so it shows your recent results in the game.

Is the IQ estimate real?

It is a rough estimate for fun. It is not a clinical IQ test. A real IQ test is given in person by a trained psychologist.

Will MindBlitz make me smarter?

MindBlitz is a game made for fun. We do not claim that it improves memory, intelligence or any other ability, or that it prevents any condition. Your scores usually rise as you get used to each game. If you are worried about your memory or thinking, please talk to a doctor.

Some games flash. Is that safe for me?

Some games use flashing tiles and quick colour changes. If you have epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing lights, talk to a doctor before you play, and stop at once if you feel unwell. See Health and Safety.

How do I add or remove a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, choose Widgets, find MindBlitz and drag the Daily Blitz or streak widget to your home screen. To remove one, touch and hold it and choose Remove.

I do not get the daily reminder

Check that the reminder is on in the app, that notifications for MindBlitz are allowed in your phone's settings, and that battery saving is not stopping the app.

How do I stop the reminder?

Turn off Daily reminder on the Me tab. You can also block MindBlitz notifications in your phone's settings.

There is no sound or vibration

Check the Sound and Vibration switches on the Me tab, and your phone's volume and Do Not Disturb setting.

I reinstalled the app or changed phones and my progress is gone

Progress is stored only on your phone. If backup is turned on, Android may restore it on a new phone. Otherwise it cannot be recovered, because we never receive it.

How do I delete my data?

Your progress is on your phone, and in your Google account only if you signed in with Google Play Games for a cloud save. Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove it from the phone, and delete the cloud save from your Play Games profile. See Delete Your Data for step-by-step help.

How do I cancel MindBlitz Plus?

Open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose MindBlitz and tap Cancel subscription. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it. Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for. See Subscriptions and Purchases.

Plus or my coins are missing after a purchase

Open the Plus screen and tap Restore purchases, and check that Google Play is signed in to the Google account you bought with. Coins you already had are stored only on your phone, so they are lost if you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. If something still looks wrong, email us your Google Play order number, which starts with GPA.

How do I get a refund?

Ask Google Play first, in your Google Play order history. Google allows refund requests for a short time after purchase. After that, email us with your order number. Subscriptions and Purchases explains refunds and the 14-day right of withdrawal in the EU and UK.

I found a wrong answer

Email us with the game and the puzzle you saw, and we will check it.
