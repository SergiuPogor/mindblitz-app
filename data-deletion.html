Delete Your Data

Last updated: 2026-11-09 · Version 1.1

MindBlitz, by MEDIAPRONET SRL, has no accounts of its own and sends nothing to us. Your progress is stored on your phone and, only if you sign in with Google Play Games, in a cloud save in your Google account. You can delete both yourself at any time, and we have no copy to delete.

What is stored

Your scores, ratings, streak, Daily Blitz and IQ Challenge results, Brain Teasers progress, badges, the names you typed for the IQ certificate or Duel, your settings, and, if you bought something, your Plus status, coin balance and the purchase records Google Play passed to the app. See the Privacy Policy for the full list.

Delete everything and keep the app

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Tap Apps, then MindBlitz. On some phones, tap See all apps first.
  3. Tap Storage and cache (or Storage).
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data), then OK.

The next time you open MindBlitz, it starts fresh. The exact names can differ a little between phone makers.

Delete everything and remove the app

Uninstall MindBlitz: touch and hold its icon and choose Uninstall, or uninstall it from Google Play. Android deletes all of the app's data with it.

Before you delete: purchases and coins

  • Coins are deleted too. Your coin balance is part of your progress. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes it from your phone, and coins you bought cannot be restored, unless you use the Play Games cloud save, which brings them back with the rest of your progress when you sign in again.
  • Plus comes back. Plus subscriptions and Plus Lifetime belong to your Google account. After clearing storage or reinstalling, open the Plus screen and tap Restore purchases.
  • Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. To stop paying, cancel in Google Play: Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then MindBlitz. See Subscriptions and Purchases.
  • Order records: Google keeps your purchase history in your Google account under its own privacy policy. We keep the limited order records Google gives us in Play Console for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

Play Games cloud save

If you signed in with Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is kept in your Google account. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling does not remove it, and signing in again brings it back. To delete it:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app, or go to https://play.google.com/games/profile in a browser.
  2. Open your profile settings and find the option to delete data for individual games, then choose MindBlitz. You can also delete your whole Play Games profile there.

The exact menu names may change, as Google runs Play Games. We cannot see or delete your cloud save for you.

Device backups

If backup is turned on for your phone, a copy of the app's progress may be in your device backup, held by Google or your device maker under your account. To remove it, turn off backup or delete the backup in your phone's settings (usually Settings, then Google, then Backup), or manage backups in your Google account. We cannot see or delete these backups for you.

Reminder and widgets

  • Reminder: turn off Daily reminder on the Me tab, or block MindBlitz notifications in your phone's settings. Clearing storage or uninstalling also stops it.
  • Widgets: touch and hold a MindBlitz widget on your home screen and choose Remove. Widgets show only data already on your phone and keep nothing of their own.

Things you shared

Pictures and texts you shared through the share menu are now in the app you shared them to, such as a chat or social app. Delete them there. A copy of the last shared picture is kept in the app's cache folder, which is removed when you clear the app's storage or cache.

Emails you sent us

If you emailed us, we hold your email address and messages. To have them deleted, email mediapronetsrl@gmail.com and ask. We delete them within one month and confirm by email, unless the law requires us to keep them.

This website

The website sets no cookies and keeps no account. Your theme choice and best demo score are stored only in your browser; clear this site's data in your browser to remove them. Our host, GitHub, keeps security logs of visitors' IP addresses under its own privacy statement; we do not receive them.
